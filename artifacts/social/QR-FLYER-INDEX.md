# Sparkle Suite Rep QR Flyers

This is the central registry for rep-specific QR flyers and their reusable source packages.

## Storage convention

- Keep each rep in `artifacts/social/<rep-or-brand>-qr-flyer/`.
- Include an upload-ready JPEG, a lossless PNG, the standalone QR image, the build source, and QR validation evidence.
- Name the primary social export `<brand>-qr-flyer.jpg` so it is easy to attach to email and repost from a phone.
- Verify the finished PNG and JPEG both decode to the intended live customer domain before delivery.
