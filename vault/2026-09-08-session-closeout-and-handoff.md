## Key decisions and lessons

1. Check and agree on show timing before domain migrations or customer-impacting
   releases. A technically deployable change can still be badly timed.
2. Distinguish source heartbeat health, server stale-data policy, and browser
   refresh behavior. Retaining old names does not make them current. Never forge
   timestamps, treat failed reads as empty success, or silently hide uncertainty.
3. Automatic data polling is different from a disruptive full-page refresh.
   Keep production-show mitigations narrow and extension work separately approved.
   Follow LIVE_EXTENSION_SAFETY.md; no local extension edits/builds/store changes.
4. Preserve bespoke rep branding and saved content with tenant-scoped presentation
   changes. Prefer a shared status notice over repetitive per-person labels.
   Check actual desktop/mobile rendering, not just build success.
5. Track exact commit/deployment/domain mappings. A push is not a manual release;
   memory-only commits are not application deployments. Other customer aliases
   do not necessarily share the newest app commit.
6. Distinguish an email draft from a sent email and a promised credit from an
   applied credit. Open Brain auto-extracted action metadata is not authorization
   to send the welcome draft, change billing, or provision real team invitations.
7. Welcome copy must distinguish existing customer-site access from new Workspace
   access, live in-app messaging from future campaigns, and beta UI from fully
   provisioned workflows. Do not publish private login/meeting details.
8. Founder count 19 is historical/time-sensitive: recheck before posting.
   33% savings applies to first12 paid months with setup/renewal terms;
   free build-queue enrollment does not reserve a discount.
