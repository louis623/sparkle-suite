# MG-01 saved-site playback on Smoke

The ordinary Fizz Fest homepage had the globe selected and Hero motion set to Still. On a normal connection the player stayed at time zero and disabled Play. The optional Smoke preview forced motion on and hid the failure.

The Smoke demo now requests motion. The globe respects Still on first paint and accepts a deliberate Play click. Save-Data and slow connections keep manual start, and reduced-motion users get the poster. The Smoke preview inherits the actual hero motion setting. For future checks, open the saved customer-site URL without preview parameters and verify time advances before clicking, then Pause and Play. Check the saved Still case separately. The two Christmas theme suites passed 14 checks. No Live release.
