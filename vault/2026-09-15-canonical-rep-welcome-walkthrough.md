# Canonical rep welcome walkthrough — September 15, 2026

## Decision

The Kelly / Sparkly Butterflies long-form Workspace walkthrough is the one
canonical public Sparkle Suite rep-welcome guide. Do not create a generic
feature-card, short starter, or checklist-first welcome-site alternative.

## Required shape

- Warm personalized hero and a visible **Take it at your pace** contents rail.
- The guided Workspace sequence: home, first visit, Workspace map, Nic-Nac,
  Calendar, Dance Floor, customer-facing site, Customer List, Messages and
  Help, Team Management only when verified, account and plan, and a small
  first-session guide.
- Common questions and a calm closing. A device-local checklist may supplement
  the walkthrough but never replace it.

## Live Queue device guidance

When Live Queue is mentioned, say plainly: it works in Google Chrome on a Mac
or Windows computer. An iPhone or iPad cannot run the browser extension. Point
the rep to Help & Resources for the setup steps. No extension, Store, or live
queue changes were made.

## Lindsey implementation

Lindsey's rebuilt, published guide uses this canonical shape and includes the
Mac/Chrome Live Queue note. Its existing unsent onboarding email draft keeps
the same stable guide link; no email was sent.
