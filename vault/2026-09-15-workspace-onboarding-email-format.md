# Workspace onboarding email format — September 15, 2026

## Decision

The Kelly Workspace walkthrough email is the canonical formatting pattern for
all Sparkle Suite rep onboarding emails. Dense, single-block drafts are not
acceptable.

## Required presentation

- Blank line between the greeting, welcome, every all-caps section heading,
  short paragraph, and sign-off.
- Sign-in details, the public-site link, personal walkthrough link, private
  meeting link, and billing route each appear on their own line.
- Keep each paragraph to one focused thought; headings must never be embedded
  in body prose.
- In Gmail, use formatting that creates actual visible paragraph breaks and
  visually inspect the saved compose window before considering a draft done.

## Scope

The account and billing section remains specific to the verified recipient:
never copy a paid-plan paragraph into a grandfathered or $0 account.
Lindsey's unsent onboarding draft was reformatted to this standard and includes
the verified Mac/Chrome Live Queue guidance. No email was sent.
