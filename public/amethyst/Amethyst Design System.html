FAQ — questions locked, answers customizable

How much does it cost to start?
The starter kit is $99 and includes everything you need for your first three live shows — product, marketing materials, training access, and one-on-one onboarding with me.
How much time do I need each week?
Most reps on my team go live 2–3 times a week for an hour each. You can absolutely do this part-time around a 9-to-5 or kids.
Do I have to recruit to make money?
Nope. Plenty of reps on my team make great income just from product sales and live shows.
