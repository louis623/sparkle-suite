So why does your storefront still look like a list of links?

If you’re treating this like a real fizbiz, your customers deserve one clear home. Not a scramble between Linktree, DMs, and “which link is the shop?”

## What live jewelry selling actually asks of you

Bomb Party reps sell surprise-reveal jewelry live on social. Shoppers jump in, grab a spot, watch the reveal, and often want to browse more or find your next show.

That means your customers need answers mid-show and between shows:

- Where do I order or find your shop?
- Where am I in line?
- What’s available to trade or browse?
- When is your next live?

A simple bio link can point somewhere. It usually can’t carry the whole show.

## The prestige gap

You’re running a high-end live show. Putting that behind a basic Linktree sends the wrong signal.

Serious shoppers notice. They notice when the brand on camera doesn’t match the page they land on. They notice when they have to hunt for the real shop in comments and DMs.

Your show deserves better than a Linktree. One polished home says you’re set up for the long haul.

## One customer home (not five apps)

Sparkle Suite gives you a real customer-facing website for your live-selling business.

Shoppers get one place to:

- Find your next show
- Follow your Live Lineup during the live
- Explore your Dance Floor

You pick themes that feel like your brand. Seasonal looks when the calendar flips. Phones, tablets, and desktop. Your colors. Your personality.

You can keep a domain you already own. We plan that on a short call.

Sparkle Suite is its own thing. It is not owned by, run by, or officially affiliated with Bomb Party. It’s built for Bomb Party reps who want their setup to match how hard they work.

## What happens during the live

**Live Lineup** keeps the line visible on your site so shoppers can follow along without you repeating names all show.

**Dance Floor** gives shoppers a place to browse pieces and send trade requests. You still make the final call.

Less “wait, which link?” in the chat. More attention on the reveal.

## What happens between shows

**Live Show Calendar** gives customers one place to see what’s coming up.

**Team Management** and private new-rep onboarding give your team a clear starting point when you’re growing.

**Nic-Nac** is there for paying reps when you need a hand with setup, content, or show prep.

Email and SMS updates for customers are coming soon. They’re not live yet.

## What Sparkle Suite does not do

It does not replace Bomb Party’s systems.

It does not process Bomb Party payments or change Party Orders.

It does not promise income, sales, recruiting, or growth numbers. Nobody should.

It’s a website and show toolkit so your brand has a proper home, and your live tools sit in one place.
