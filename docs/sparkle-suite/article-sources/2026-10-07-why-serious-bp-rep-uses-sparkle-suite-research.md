### Linktree / link-in-bio vs owned site — general industry (careful; no fake stats)

Use as **supporting reason**, not as Suite-owned proof:

- Live shopping often still runs on a **static** link-in-bio; static lists don’t update with show state / inventory timing; teams fall back to chat / copy-paste URLs mid-stream (industry blog framing).  
  Source: https://lin.ky/i/blog/live-shopping-bio-links-2025

- Link-in-bio is a router / hallway; an owned site can hold brand, calendar, content that compounds for search; bio tools on third-party domains don’t build your SEO the same way.  
  Sources: https://spurlockstudios.com/blog/linktree-is-a-leak-build-the-house · https://www.cacele.com/resources/linktree-vs-your-own-website · https://usebuildify.com/post/linktree-vs-custom-website-creators
