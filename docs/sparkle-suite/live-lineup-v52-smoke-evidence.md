## Timing and compatibility contract

Accepted upgraded source evidence expires after 45 seconds; a ready source lease is at most 90 seconds. An observation must be no older than 15 seconds at commit. Non-ready packets shorten the current lease to no later than 15 seconds after their receipt and do not extend it. Workspace keeps its last-good rows while controls are locked. Public retention is at most 3,600 seconds from the last genuine ready source observation; after expiry it says it is waiting for a recent update. Returning data establishes an animation baseline. Workspace/public clients conservatively subtract the complete request round trip; received responses do not restart evidence lifetime. Hidden/resumed documents must lock before fetching.

Missing public capability remains legacy first-name/per-order presentation. New Home/Join/Trade documents opt into `lineupPresentation=grouped-v1` for initial payload and polls. Already-open legacy customer pages require a customer-site refresh for the new visuals; BP needs no refresh for presentation. Existing 2.0.4 publishers remain supported without surname eligibility or unreveal authority. Assigned-code legacy configure can return a compatibility no-op before a lease exists; 2.0.5 requires exact claim/epoch proof and an applied-scope receipt. Legacy configure cannot change an upgraded lease. No removal date for compatibility has been approved.

Automatic unreveal restores only an order with saved prior membership and a newer accepted unchecked observation in the same source document, show and publisher epoch. A previously unseen already-checked order has no prior placement to restore: it never flares, and unchecked evidence alone does not resurrect it. Intentional new-show review is the recovery path. This limitation is deliberate; the build does not fabricate historical membership or customer identity.

The private candidate state is bounded at 8 MiB, 2,000 active-plus-held orders and 10,000 revealed/restoration records. Recent event history retains at most 128 events and 4,000 total original-group member references; it drops whole oldest event records without truncating group membership or changing customers. Cursor gaps never fabricate effects; the latest explicitly retained valid event may still animate once. Source-document retirement history is capped at 32 IDs within one publisher epoch; exceeding that bound fails closed with `capacity_exceeded`, retaining last-good state until a fresh claim after lease expiry. That conservative long-session replacement limit requires operational observation rather than an unlimited replacement claim.
