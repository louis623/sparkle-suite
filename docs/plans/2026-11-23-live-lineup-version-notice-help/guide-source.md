## What's new
- Start Show and Stop Show buttons. They're in the extension and on your Workspace Live Lineup card. Press Start and your site says "I have an open lineup." right away, even before the first order. Press Stop and it goes back to "Check the calendar for the next show." with your next scheduled show listed.
- It wraps up on its own if you forget. If you don't press Stop, the lineup ends when your party closes, or after 2 hours with no new orders.
- Updates install themselves. Every time Chrome starts, the extension checks for an update and installs it. Your version number and a Check for updates button are right in the extension.
- Clearer status in your Workspace. The card shows your extension version and whether it's connected. If something's wrong, it turns red and says Needs attention. Tap it to see what's going on. "Reconnecting" only shows in your Workspace; your customers just see that the lineup is updating automatically.
- Customers with more than one order stay together. They show as one row with the order count, move together when you drag them, and you tap the count to see each order.
- Customer card. Tap it to see and save private notes, a birthday, and favorites like stone, metal, cut and collection. It's private to your Workspace.
- Easier on your phone. The order count and Customer card sit together on the right, the Customer card button is bigger to tap, and grouped rows no longer pop open or closed on their own.

## How to run your show with Live Lineup

### Before you go live
1. On your computer, open Chrome and go to your Bomb Party Live Party Orders page. Keep just one of those tabs open. Opening Chrome a few minutes early gives any update time to install.
2. Click the Sparkle Suite extension. It should say Connected + Updating and Extension {LIVE_LINEUP_RELEASE.version}. First time only, enter the Live Lineup code shown at the top of your Workspace.
3. Under Detected parties, make sure today's party is checked. Uncheck any party you don't want on your site.
4. Press Start Show, in the extension or on your Workspace card. Your site now says "I have an open lineup." and the extension shows when the lineup ends. If you start from your Workspace, the extension can take about 30 seconds to catch up.

[Image 01 after step 4]

### During your show
5. Keep your Workspace Live Lineup card open on your phone or computer. You'll see Live and Connected, and orders come in the same order as Bomb Party.
6. Use Reveal next, Hold, or drag a name to change the order. Undo takes back your last change. None of this changes your Bomb Party orders.
7. Tap Customer card to see a customer's private notes, birthday and favorites, or to add them.
8. If the status turns red and says Needs attention, tap it to see why. Most of the time the Live Party Orders page closed or reloaded. Keep that page open and Live Lineup reconnects on its own.

[Images 02 and 03 side by side after step 8; stacked on phones]

### When you're done
9. Press Stop Show. Your site goes back to "Check the calendar for the next show." Forgot? It ends on its own when your party closes or after 2 hours with no new orders.
10. Keep your next show on your Workspace Calendar so it shows up under that message.

[Image 04 after step 10]

## If your Workspace says your extension is out of date (anchor id: update-extension)
**Don't remove the extension. Removing it erases your Live Lineup connection code.**
1. On your computer, open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions.
2. Turn on Developer mode (top right), press Update, then turn Developer mode back off.
3. Open your Bomb Party Live Party Orders page. The notice clears once the extension reports the new version.

On 2.0.13 or newer, this usually happens by itself when Chrome starts, and the extension has a Check for updates button. Updates can take a few hours to show up after we release one.

## Need a hand?
If anything looks off, tap Report a bug at the top of your Workspace or message us through Workspace Support. [Button: reuse onContactSupport; text only in the support view.]
