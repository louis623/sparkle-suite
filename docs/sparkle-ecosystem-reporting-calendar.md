# Sparkle ecosystem reporting calendar

Sparkle Suite and Sparkle Finder use the same reporting-week convention:

- A week starts Sunday and ends Saturday.
- Scheduled weekly reports are due in the rep's Sparkle Suite Message Center by 6:00 a.m. Sunday in `America/New_York`.
- Birthday data is month and day only. Birth years are never collected, stored, imported, displayed, or published.
- February 29 birthdays are observed on February 28 in non-leap years.

This shared calendar is a product convention only. Sparkle Suite and Sparkle Finder keep separate customer data, authentication, databases, and deployments unless a separately approved integration changes that boundary.
