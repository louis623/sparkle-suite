# Social brand marks (business card back)

The TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube path data in `bizcard-render.ts`
(`BRAND_ICONS`) comes from Simple Icons v16.34.0 (https://simpleicons.org),
released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md

The marks are drawn monochrome in the theme's icon color. The brands themselves
are trademarks of their owners; they are used only to label the rep's own handle.
