# Macie's Christmas Globe (MG-01)

The approved Main Gem Co. hero is the text-free landscape plate. The portrait edit supplies the evergreen background of the QR flyer. The landscape supplies its photographic footer and the business card front. Run `node scripts/card-qr/plate-sources/macies-christmas-globe/compose.mjs` from the project root to rebuild the print plates and thumbnail.

The direct Higgsfield image-to-video request completed once. Its pre-discount estimate was $4.622 under a $15 cap. To rebuild both silent H.264 loops, run `python finish-motion.py <original-API-take.mp4> <hero-poster.webp> <output-directory>` from this folder with FFmpeg, Pillow and NumPy installed. The 220 frames at 24 fps retain the original blinks and falling snow. A gentle head tilt, separate flame movement and slowly fading warm tree bulbs repeat over the same period, with a forward overlap on the original take. The jewelry and packaging stay fixed. The 640 × 360 phone encode follows the identical motion; music is independent.

WaveMaster's *We Wish You a Merry Christmas Music Box* plays once per wind-up, separate from the visual loop. Review the track and license at [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/music/christmas-we-wish-you-a-merry-christmas-music-box-175846/) and the [Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/). Use the recording within the themed customer experience. Font license notices accompany the web and print files.

The normal print renderers supply the text, scannable QR codes and PDF trim and bleed. Run `node --import tsx scripts/macies-globe-review.ts <absolute-output-directory>` for a local Home proof, PNG and JPEG flyer, both card faces and two-page CMYK PDF. The review HTML disables submissions and navigation. Its sample lineup is synthetic.

The catalog migration verifies both the rep UUID and public slug before issuing the theme. The existing RPC controls owner and internal-demo access. Environments without the owner have no issued row. No saved appearance is updated.
