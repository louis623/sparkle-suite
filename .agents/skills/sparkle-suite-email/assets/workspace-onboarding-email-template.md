# Workspace onboarding email template

Use this structure for every Sparkle Suite rep Workspace welcome or walkthrough email. Replace only verified recipient-specific facts. Keep credentials, private meeting links, and payment details in the email draft—not a public guide.

```text
Hi {{REP_FIRST_NAME}},

Welcome to Sparkle Suite — I’m excited to get {{BUSINESS_NAME}} set up with you.

Here’s everything you’ll need for our first walkthrough:

YOUR SPARKLE SUITE WORKSPACE

Sign in: {{WORKSPACE_URL}}
Username: {{RECIPIENT_EMAIL}}
{{PASSWORD_OR_SIGN_IN_HELP}}

{{ONE_SHORT_ACCESS_SUPPORT_PARAGRAPH}}

YOUR CUSTOMER-FACING SITE

Your public customer site is live here:
{{PUBLIC_SITE_URL}}

YOUR PERSONAL WELCOME GUIDE

I made a walkthrough just for {{BUSINESS_NAME}}. You can open it anytime here:
{{WELCOME_GUIDE_URL}}

YOUR MEETING ROOM

We’ll walk through everything together here:
{{PRIVATE_MEETING_URL}}

{{ONE_SHORT_WALKTHROUGH_PARAGRAPH}}

There is no homework before we meet.

LIVE QUEUE ON A MAC

When you are ready for a live show, Live Queue works in Google Chrome on a Mac or Windows computer. An iPhone or iPad cannot run the browser extension. Help & Resources has the setup steps when you are ready.

YOUR {{PLAN_LABEL}} PLAN & BILLING

{{ONE_SHORT_VERIFIED_ENTITLEMENT_PARAGRAPH}}

{{ONE_SHORT_VERIFIED_PAYMENT_OR_NO_PAYMENT_PARAGRAPH}}

{{BILLING_ROUTE_IF_RELEVANT}}

I’m looking forward to building this with you.

Respectfully,
Louis Chapman
Sparkle Suite
(720) 629-6507
louis@yoursparklesuite.com
```

## Final visual check

The draft must visibly show the separate paragraphs and headings above in Gmail. A screen-reader text value or source string is not enough if the compose window visually renders it as a wall of text.
