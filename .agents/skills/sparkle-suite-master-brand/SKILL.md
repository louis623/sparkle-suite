## Working process

- Read sources, then draft from supported claims and the actual channel goal.

- Review against 08 and the checklist; explain meaningful conflicts plainly.
- Report whether a result is a concept, local build, Smoke preview, or Live release accurately.

## Rules that survive every channel

- Sparkle Suite is a standalone rep brand, not a Neon Rabbit agency offer.
- Warm plum `#34252f` anchors the identity; espresso `#36221d` frames proof; warm paper/blush give breathing room; pink marks actions. Use Playfair Display / DM Sans.
- Use the approved PNG logo lockup and locked white footer. Do not invent a knockout logo or use Finder violet for Suite chrome.
- Current names: Dance Floor, Live Lineup, event calendar, themes. Say “themes,” never “skins,” to users.
- Nic-Nac is the included paid-rep assistant. No public marketing launcher, anonymous chat, or FAQ handoff form. One factual feature line is okay.
- Customer email/SMS updates are coming soon; no invented date or availability.
- CTA: Join the build queue. No payment at signup; Louis emails for a quick 30-minute call; build starts after first month and setup are paid. Queue signup does not reserve founder pricing.
- Follow exact pricing and counter rules in 08. Never fabricate scarcity, reviews, testimonials, customers, or outcomes.
- No Bomb Party headline or added affiliation/permission pitch; preserve the locked footer disclaimer and approved founder story.
- Real-site labels use show name/domain only, never personal rep names. Product previews may show each site's own theme; page chrome remains Suite.
- Avoid generic software jargon, hype, AI spectacle, and vague polished-sounding filler. Explain a concrete customer experience.
