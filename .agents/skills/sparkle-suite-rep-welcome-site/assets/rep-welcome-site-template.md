# Canonical Rep Workspace Walkthrough

This is the single Sparkle Suite rep-welcome guide format. It is the approved Kelly / Sparkly Butterflies long-form walkthrough, with rep-specific facts substituted only where verified. Do not create a shorter card-based, generic, checklist-first, or marketing-style alternative.

Never put a password, email address, private Live Queue code, shipping address, payment-link URL, account ID, or private meeting URL in the public guide.

## Required guide shape

1. A warm hero with the rep's first name and business, two actions (start the walkthrough and open the verified Workspace/customer site), and the exact reassurance: reading ahead is welcome, but **no homework is required**.
2. A visible contents rail titled **Take it at your pace**.
3. These long-form sections, in order: **Your Sparkle Suite home**; **Your first visit**; **Where do I go to do that?**; **Meet Nic-Nac**; **Plan your next show**; **Your Dance Floor**; **Make your website feel like you**; **Keep your customer details together**; **Find a conversation. Get an answer.**; **Your Team Management tools** only when verified; **Your account & plan**; and **Keep your first session small**.
4. A required **Live Queue on a Mac** note near the Workspace map or first-session section: Live Queue works in Google Chrome on a Mac or Windows computer; an iPhone or iPad cannot run the browser extension. Point the rep to Help & Resources for setup steps.
5. **A few common questions**: the guide does not change or prove setup, screens can look different, and help is available.
6. A calm closing with the verified Workspace/customer-site link.

Do not collapse the sequence into a feature grid or replace it with a generic first-week checklist. Team Management is omitted cleanly when it is not verified. The account section uses only verified terms and `Tools → Account → Billing Access` for Stripe management; a $0 grandfathered account plainly says no payment action is required.

## Visual and interaction rules

- Use the Kelly guide's editorial long-form hierarchy: generous reading width, numbered section markers, subtle information boxes, expandable details, a two-column map, and a contents rail. It is a calm private walkthrough, not a marketing landing page.
- A device-local checklist is optional supporting material only. It must not replace the walkthrough or suggest completion changes Sparkle Suite.
- Keep navigation links real and verified. Exercise at least one during smoke.
- Keep credentials and private meeting details in the unsent email, not here.
